Linkquisy ("we") is operated by [your name / organisation], [contact email].

**What we collect**
- Account details: your name and email address.
- Messages you send and the chats you belong to, so they can be delivered to the other members.
- Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (Google Firebase) for security and troubleshooting.

**The Librarian bot.** When a message in a chat contains a paper or book reference, the text of that reference
(for example a DOI or a title) is sent to public open-access databases (Unpaywall, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, arXiv,
DOAJ, CORE, OAPEN, DOAB, Project Gutenberg) to find a free legal copy. No other part of your message, and
nothing that identifies you, is sent. You can turn the bot off for any chat.

**How we use it.** Only to run the app. We don't sell your data or show ads.

**Where it is stored.** On Google Firebase servers in [region]. Data is encrypted in transit.

**Deleting your data.** In the app [Settings > Delete account], or email [contact email]. We delete your account
and profile within 30 days. Messages you sent in groups may stay visible to the other members.

**Children.** Linkquisy is not meant for children under 13.

**Changes.** We'll update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
