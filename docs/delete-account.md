# Delete your Linquisy account

**In the app:** open Linquisy, tap the gear icon (Settings), then **Delete account** and confirm.

**Without the app:** email [your contact email] from the address you signed up with, with the subject
"Delete my Linquisy account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

**What is deleted:** your profile (name and email), your sign-in account, your notification device IDs, and your
membership of every chat.

**What is kept:** messages you already sent in group chats stay visible to the other members. Reports you filed are kept for up to 90 days for safety reviews.
